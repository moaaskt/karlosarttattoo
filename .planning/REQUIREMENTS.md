# Requirements

## Functional Requirements
- **Hero Section**: A video background hero section with distinct calls to action for different booking types (Studio, Home, Flash/Workshop).
- **Location Section**: Distinct information cards for the locations served by the artist.
- **Portfolio Section**: A dynamic masonry/grid view of selected tattoo works. Hover effects should reveal Instagram links and captions.
- **Booking Modal**: An integrated modal form capturing Name, Phone, Email, Location of service, and a brief description of the idea. Data is sent to Formspree.

## Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: High performance with SSR, lazy loading where appropriate, and smooth 60fps animations.
- **Aesthetics**: Dark mode exclusive. "Editorial" feel with wide letter spacing, uppercase typography, and subtle micro-animations.
- **Responsiveness**: Fully responsive from mobile devices up to large desktop screens.
